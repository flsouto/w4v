# Looplicator DAW 3.3: meet Glitch, load packs, shape your mosaics

*Published with v3.3.0 · 2026-11-07*

A new Glitch mixer, store packs inside the DAW, and an expanded mosaic library: 430 patterns instead of 86, plus custom patterns, previews, and waveform markers.

More ways to start, more ways to rearrange, and a new mixer to shake things up. Looplicator DAW 3.3 brings Glitch into the browser, connects the sample library to the Looplicator store, and gives mosaic a much larger pattern collection with room for your own experiments.



## Meet Glitch, the eighth mixer

Glitch joins the mixer lineup with a taste for chopped rhythms and unexpected movement. It pulls pieces from two source loops, switches between them, and reshapes them with stutters, rising pitch shifts, and stretched passages. The result repeats as a cycle you can play, process, and feed back into the library.

Choose **glitch** in the Mixer menu and generate a few variations. Familiar samples can take on a very different character.



## Mosaic grows from 86 to 430 patterns

Mosaic now has **430 unique patterns, up from 86** in the previous release. That is 344 additional sequences for rearranging your samples, with all patterns enabled by default.

You can also choose which patterns mosaic uses. Keep a small selection for a particular feel, or leave the whole collection open to chance.

Want to go further? Write your own patterns, preview them before adding them, and edit or remove your experiments whenever you like. Each letter identifies a sample slice; an underscore adds silence. Your custom patterns and selection preferences stay saved in your browser.

Every mosaic generation now labels its steps directly on the waveform. Follow the letters and silence markers across the loop, hear how a sequence takes shape, and use that feedback to refine the next one.



## Bring store packs straight into the DAW

The library’s **+** button now offers **Load from my computer** or **Load from Looplicator store**. Browse dated packs with cover thumbnails, choose one yourself, or hit **Load random pack** for a surprise starting point.

Tracks download and convert to WAV one at a time, appearing in your library as they finish. A compact progress panel lets you keep playing, generating, and adjusting the controls while the rest of the pack loads.

You can stop an import and keep the tracks already added. If a download fails, loading the pack again retries missing tracks and skips those already in your library. Free store samples can be included in sample ZIP downloads; paid store samples are excluded.



## A clearer view of stereo loops

The waveform now reflects both stereo channels, so audio remains visible even when one side is quiet or delayed.
